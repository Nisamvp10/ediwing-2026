Registration Process

We offer a seamless path to your educational journey.

Applicants can apply online, call our counselors, or chat with us to learn about policies, but must submit a correctly completed and self-attested admission form along with all required documents and fees via mail or courier to complete the process.

01
Eligibility

Please verify that you meet all academic and professional requirements to ensure you are eligible for the course

02
Application Submission

Complete your online application and submit all required documents to begin the process

03
Screening & Shortlisting

Your application will be reviewed against established criteria to shortlist eligible candidates.

04
Admission & Fee Payment

Selected candidates receive confirmation and complete fee payment.

05
Book Your Seat

Confirm your seat to finalize enrollment and secure your place in the program.

Mandatory Documents

Study At Your Pace With Globally Recognized Online

To process your admission for High School, Higher Secondary, Diploma, Bachelor, Master, or PhD programs, the following documents are required:

  • Completed Admission Form
  • Valid copy of Passport (Front & Back)
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Academic Certificates and Marksheets (10th, 12th, Diploma, Bachelor’s, Master’s – as applicable)
  • Updated Resume / CV (for higher-level programs)
  • Work Experience Certificate (if applicable)
  • A photocopy of a government-issued photo ID (such as a passport, driver’s licence, voter ID card, Aadhaar card, or PAN card).
  • Research Proposal / Statement of Purpose (for Master’s / PhD, if required)
  • Valid ID Proof (National ID)
  • Fee Receipt
  • Please visit the official website of the respective university for more detailed information.

Important Notes

  • All documents must be clear and legible.
  • Documents should be submitted in PDF or JPEG format.
  • Additional documents may be requested based on the selected program or university requirements.
  • Attested documents may be required depending on the country and course.

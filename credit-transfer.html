Credit Transfer at Eduwing Education

Programs designed to accelerate your
career growth - Credit Transfer

Credit transfer is an academic process that enables students to move from one institution to another while preserving the credits they have already earned. Instead of repeating completed subjects, students can continue their studies from where they left off.

Credit Transfer is a degree program where students can complete the complete 2, 3 or 4 year course in a single examination. The student is awarded a degree upon completing the course.

The process generally includes a detailed evaluation of previously completed courses reviewing the syllabus, grades, learning outcomes, and subject relevance to ensure they align with the equivalency standards and eligibility criteria of the new institution.

Eligibility Criteria for Credit Transfer Degree | Degree Completion Programs

Candidates with interruptions in their academic journey are eligible to apply under this pathway, subject to meeting any of the following criteria:

  • Students who have discontinued their education during the first year, second year, or third year of their program are eligible for this process.
  • Students who have successfully completed their Higher Secondary or Graduation can pursue higher qualifications through Degree Completion Programs.
  • The last completed level of education must be from a government-recognized university or institution.
  • Students are provided the option to pursue their studies through classroom or distance learning modes, subject to submission of valid proof of their last completed qualification.
  • Based on their previous qualifications, students will be guided to apply for lateral entry or to progress to the next appropriate academic level.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  • What is the inter-university credit transfer ?
    Inter-university credit transfer allows students to transfer academic credits earned from one recognized university to another, so they do not need to repeat courses already completed.
  • Who is Eligible for Credit Transfer ?
    Credit transfer is generally available at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Students must typically have completed at least one year or a certain percentage Course (usually 50%) of their program to be eligible.
  • What Documents Are Required?
    Academic transcripts/mark sheets Syllabus or course description from the former university, preferably from the same year the course was completed The application form for credit transfer.
  • How Many Credits Can Be Transferred?
    The maximum transferable credits depend on the receiving university’s policy, but core subjects typically must be completed at the new (host) university. Students may transfer only a fixed percentage of total program credits (as each university sets its upper credit transfer limit and equivalency rules).
  • What is the Usual Process?
    Provide required documents (transcripts, course descriptions, syllabus).The receiving university reviews and evaluates the credits for equivalency.

    The university communicates the credits accepted the student continues studies from there.
  • what is The Course Duration when Transfer Course Credit from one university to another university?
    The process can take from few Months to years, depending on documentation, university workload, and the complexity of credit equivalency evaluation.
  • What are the Benefits of Credit Transfer?
    Students can complete Degree Without repeating subjects which already passed. It Reduce tuition Fees and study duration Flexibility to change universities due to personal or academic reasons Acknowledgment of prior learning and effort.
  • Does Credit Transfer Impact Employability?
    As long as the final degree is from a UGC recognized /accredited university, transferred credits do not affect government or private sector job eligibility in India or abroad.
  • Are There Limitations?
    Not all courses are eligible (core and highly specialized subjects may not transfer).Only credits from recognized/approved institutions are considered.The actual number of credits accepted is at the discretion of the receiving university.

Navigating International Recognition: WES, NZQA, NAARIC, and More

As you embark on an international academic journey, ensuring the recognition of your credentials is paramount. Verification / Evaluation body such as WES, NZQA, and NAARIC play a pivotal role in validating and assessing your qualifications. Here’s an overview of these organizations and their significance:

World Education Services

WES evaluates and authenticates international credentials for use in educational and professional settings. If you’re pursuing opportunities abroad, obtaining a WES evaluation can help institutions understand and assess your academic achievements from the university.

New Zealand Qualifications Authority

NZQA is crucial if you’re considering studying or working in New Zealand. They assess and compare international qualifications against the New Zealand Qualifications Framework, aiding in admissions and employment processes.

National Academic Recognition Information Centre

NAARIC serves as a valuable resource for credential evaluation in North America. If you’re planning to study or work in the United States or Canada, NAARIC evaluations offer insights into the comparability of your credentials.

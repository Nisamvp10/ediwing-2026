About the Role

We are looking for a passionate and student-focused Academic Counselor to guide prospective and enrolled students in making informed academic decisions. The ideal candidate will play a key role in helping students understand our programs, align their goals with the right courses, and ensure a smooth enrollment journey.


If you are a strong communicator who genuinely enjoys mentoring and supporting students, this role is for you.

Key Responsibilities

  • Counsel prospective students on academic programs, career paths, and admission processes
  • Understand student goals and recommend suitable courses
  • Handle inquiries via phone, email, and in-person consultations
  • Follow up with leads and maintain accurate student records
  • Support students throughout the enrollment process
  • Coordinate with academic and administrative teams
  • Conduct orientation sessions or information seminars when required
  • Maintain monthly admission targets and reporting

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in any relevant field
  • 1–3 years of experience in academic counseling, admissions, or sales (preferred)
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to build trust and maintain student relationships
  • Goal-oriented with strong organizational skills
  • Proficiency in MS Office and CRM systems
  • Positive attitude and professional approach
